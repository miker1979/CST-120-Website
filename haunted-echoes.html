Pre-Production Concept

Untitled Arizona Paranormal Project

A cooperative mystery-horror experience set in a haunted Arizona mining town. Each playthrough selects locations from a larger pool, rearranges evidence, and changes supernatural encounters.

The team must gather enough proof to reconstruct the haunting while possession, voice mimicry, limited backtracking, and randomized clue placement make every investigation feel different.

Current Concept

  • One-to-four-player cooperative investigation
  • Eight possible locations with four selected per run
  • Randomized clues, evidence locations, and supernatural events
  • Possession, EVP markers, and voice-mimic systems
  • Locations cannot be revisited unless the story allows it
  • AI-directed encounters that react to player performance
  • Player-specific, group, and sacrifice endings

Historical Horror Concept

The Boston Ripper: Blood Before Liberty

A grounded murder-mystery horror game set in Boston during 1774–1775, as the city moves toward revolution and a ritual killer exploits the growing unrest.

Players investigate crime scenes manually, question suspects, build an evidence board, and decide which clues can be trusted before political pressure forces the case toward an ending.

Current Concept

  • Open-city historical investigation across colonial Boston
  • Customizable investigator name and personal background
  • Manual crime-scene searches and evidence-board connections
  • Partially randomized killer identity and suspect behavior
  • Conflicting testimony, uncertain clues, and false leads
  • British authority and colonial unrest affect the investigation
  • Multiple political, personal, and case-resolution endings

The Crypt Concept

Whiteout Protocol

Six investigators arrive at a frozen research base to discover what happened to the previous crew. Everyone begins human, but the game AI eventually creates a hidden infection event somewhere inside the base.

The infected player slowly loses control through subtle camera turns, unexplained controller vibration, strange impulses, and involuntary actions before the organism fully emerges.

Current Concept

  • Six-player cooperative and asymmetric multiplayer
  • Soldier, doctor, mechanic, and specialist classes
  • AI-directed infection placed during exploration
  • Investigation into the missing previous crew
  • Dynamic day/night cycle, snowstorms, and blizzards
  • Sub-zero survival, heat, visibility, and equipment systems
  • The creature grows stronger as players are eliminated

Paranormal Project Direction

Investigation should create uncertainty.

Players should not receive the same clues, threats, locations, or ending every time they begin an investigation.

The project is being designed around incomplete information, conflicting accounts, supernatural deception, limited time, and decisions that alter what the group discovers.

1

Choose the Investigation

Players select from multiple haunted locations instead of visiting every site in one session.

2

Gather Connected Evidence

Clues, stories, objects, and supernatural events reveal different pieces of the mystery.

3

Survive the Deception

Possession, mimicry, and unreliable information make it difficult to know which characters or players can be trusted.

4

Shape the Ending

Evidence, location choices, survival, and group decisions determine the final confrontation and outcome.
