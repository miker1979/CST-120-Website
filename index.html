Development Incubator

Ideas begin in Ghostline Labs.

Ghostline Labs gives research, academic projects, technical experiments, game prototypes, and early product concepts a clear home.

A project remains in Labs until its purpose, audience, technical direction, and long-term value are clear.

1

Research

Define the problem, audience, requirements, or gameplay concept.

2

Prototype

Build the smallest useful version and test its technical direction.

3

Evaluate

Review usability, feasibility, audience value, and required resources.

4

Graduate or Archive

Move successful work into Ghostline Software or Haunted Echoes Studios, or preserve it as a completed experiment.

Software Prototypes

Fleet tools, inventory applications, scheduling concepts, transportation systems, and proof-of-concept business software.

Game Prototypes

Unity experiments, game mechanics, multiplayer systems, environments, and early narrative concepts.

Academic Archive

Selected coursework that demonstrates technical growth without being presented as a commercial Ghostline product.
