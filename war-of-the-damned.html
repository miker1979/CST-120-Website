Current Build

Classic War, rebuilt as a horror experience.

War of the Damned began as a recovered Windows Forms prototype and has been rebuilt into a complete playable card game with a dedicated visual identity, automatic opponent, full War resolution, match history, and tactile card audio.

Complete War Rules

Normal clashes award both cards to the winner. Ties trigger WAR, build a shared pot, and continue until one side claims everything at stake.

The Living vs. The Damned

A single-player presentation turns the ordinary card game into a ritualized contest between two opposing factions.

Card Audio

Shuffle, draw, flip, place, and collect effects give each round a physical tabletop feel. A deeper horror sound layer is planned for a later pass.

Implemented

Current Feature Set

  • 52-card shuffled deck with 26 cards per side at the start of a ritual
  • Manual draw from the Living side or the Draw From the Abyss control
  • Automatic Damned opponent
  • Full WAR pot resolution, including repeated ties
  • Auto-War mode for rapid simulations
  • Round counter, card counts, pot size, and WAR multiplier
  • Blood Ledger round history
  • New Ritual reset flow
  • Card shuffle, draw, flip, place, and collect sound effects

Next Development Pass

Atmosphere & presentation

Planned upgrades include a visible WAR sequence, face-down deck interaction, card movement and flip animation, horror stingers, ambient sound, escalating tension as a side nears defeat, and end-of-game statistics.
