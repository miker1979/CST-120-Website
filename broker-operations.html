Product Overview

Keep every load connected from customer request to final payment.

Ghostline Broker Operations is intended to give freight brokers one organized system for managing the people, documents, financial details, appointments, and communication connected to each load.

REL

Manage Relationships

Preserve accurate customer, shipper, receiver, carrier, driver, and contact information.

OPS

Coordinate Operations

Track the complete load from the original customer request through pickup, transit, delivery, documentation, and closure.

FIN

Understand the Numbers

Connect customer revenue, carrier cost, additional charges, invoice status, and gross margin to the correct load.

Intended Users

Built for the internal side of freight brokerage.

The system is designed for people responsible for securing freight, coordinating transportation, managing relationships, protecting documents, and tracking financial results.

  • Freight brokers
  • Brokerage owners
  • Account managers
  • Carrier representatives
  • Load coordinators
  • Document specialists
  • Billing personnel
  • Operations managers

Core Brokerage Modules

The relationships, freight, documents, and money connected.

Each module supports a specific brokerage responsibility while remaining connected to the complete load record.

CUST

Customers and Locations

Organize the businesses purchasing transportation services and their operational locations.

  • Customer profiles
  • Billing contacts
  • Shipper and receiver locations
  • Appointment instructions
CARR

Carriers and Drivers

Maintain the transportation providers available to cover customer freight.

  • Carrier profiles
  • Equipment types
  • Driver contacts
  • Service areas
LOAD

Load Coordination

Preserve the complete operational record for each freight movement.

  • Origin and destination
  • Pickup and delivery appointments
  • Commodity and weight
  • Status and assigned carrier
DOC

Documents and Compliance

Keep operational and qualification documents connected to the correct carrier or load.

  • Rate confirmations
  • Carrier packets
  • Insurance records
  • Bills of lading and delivery documents
COM

Communication History

Record important conversations, updates, delays, changes, and follow-up actions.

  • Customer notes
  • Carrier communication
  • Appointment updates
  • Issue and resolution history
MARGIN

Payments and Margins

Understand the financial result associated with each completed load.

  • Customer revenue
  • Carrier cost
  • Additional charges
  • Gross margin

Brokerage Workflow

Follow the load from request to settlement.

Every operational event, document, cost, and payment should remain connected to the original customer load.

1

Create the Customer Load

Record the customer, locations, appointments, commodity, equipment, customer rate, and requirements.

2

Qualify and Assign a Carrier

Review equipment, availability, qualification documents, service area, and carrier cost.

3

Monitor Pickup and Delivery

Track appointments, check calls, delays, changes, documentation, and final delivery.

4

Close and Settle the Load

Confirm documents, customer billing, carrier payment, additional charges, and gross margin.

Relationship Management

Freight brokerage depends on reliable relationships.

Customer and carrier records should provide useful operational history, not simply names and telephone numbers.

Customer Relationships

Understand the Customer Account

  • Primary and alternate contacts
  • Shipping and receiving locations
  • Common lanes and equipment needs
  • Appointment and facility instructions
  • Load and revenue history
  • Communication and issue notes
Carrier Relationships

Understand the Carrier Network

  • Authority and qualification information
  • Insurance status
  • Equipment and operating areas
  • Driver and dispatcher contacts
  • Load and performance history
  • Communication and service notes

Financial Visibility

Revenue is only part of the result.

The system should make it easier to understand what the customer owes, what the carrier is paid, and what remains after direct load costs.

REV

Customer Revenue

Track the agreed customer rate, accessorial charges, corrections, invoice amount, and payment status.

COST

Carrier Cost

Record the carrier agreement, detention, layover, unloading, additional charges, and payment status.

GM

Gross Margin

Compare customer revenue against carrier and direct load costs to understand the financial result.

Related Ghostline Product

Broker Operations and the Ghostline Load Board

The two products serve different purposes but may eventually support a connected freight workflow.

Ghostline Load Board is intended to support freight discovery, posting, carrier matching, lane comparisons, and operating-cost awareness.

Ghostline Broker Operations is intended to manage the private internal work that occurs after a customer load enters the brokerage system.
