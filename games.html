A cooperative mystery-horror game set in a haunted Arizona mining town. Players investigate abandoned locations, uncover conflicting stories, encounter supernatural threats, and make choices that determine the final enemy and ending.

Genre: Cooperative Mystery Horror

Players: 1–4

Engine: Unreal Engine

Status: Early Concept Development

Our Development Focus

The gaming division is being designed around systems that make exploration, character decisions, environments, and story outcomes work together.

Meaningful Choices

Player decisions can affect available allies, locations, threats, story information, and final outcomes.

Atmospheric Worlds

Locations are designed to communicate history, mystery, and danger through exploration and environmental storytelling.

Replayable Stories

Different locations, discoveries, characters, and decisions can create new experiences during later playthroughs.
