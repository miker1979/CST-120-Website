Product Overview

Crews, assets, and projects in one system.

Ghostline Construction Ops is designed to replace scattered spreadsheets, text messages, paper forms, notebooks, and disconnected records with one practical operational workspace.

FIELD

Built for Field Teams

Support the employees, foremen, drivers, mechanics, operators, and managers responsible for keeping daily work moving.

REAL

Based on Real Operations

Workflows are shaped by practical transportation, construction, dispatch, maintenance, and equipment-management experience.

SCALE

Designed to Grow

Begin with essential operational tools and add capabilities as actual company requirements are identified and tested.

Intended Users

Built for companies that manage work beyond the office.

The product is intended for organizations that must coordinate employees, vehicles, equipment, materials, locations, maintenance, and changing daily assignments.

  • Small construction companies
  • Highway and barrier contractors
  • Field-service organizations
  • Equipment operators
  • Fleet and dispatch teams
  • Maintenance departments
  • Specialty contractors
  • Growing operational companies

Core Operational Modules

The essential parts of field operations, connected.

Each module supports a specific responsibility while sharing information with the rest of the operations system.

CREW

Employees and Crews

Maintain employee records and organize the people available for field assignments.

  • Employees and roles
  • Foremen and crew assignments
  • Drivers and operators
  • Availability and responsibility
FLEET

Fleet and Equipment

Track the vehicles and equipment needed to complete work safely and efficiently.

  • Trucks and work vehicles
  • Trailers
  • Forklifts and gradalls
  • Status and availability
JOB

Jobs and Tasks

Organize projects, locations, contractors, dates, work requirements, and task activity.

  • Job and project records
  • Contractors and locations
  • Start and finish dates
  • Work types and quantities
DSP

Dispatch and Assignments

Connect employees, vehicles, equipment, materials, and tasks before work begins.

  • Driver and vehicle assignments
  • Crew selection
  • Equipment placement
  • Daily dispatch planning
MAINT

Maintenance and Inspections

Preserve equipment history and identify mechanical concerns before they become larger operational problems.

  • DVIR inspections
  • Mechanical write-ups
  • Service history
  • Repair and availability status
MAT

Materials and Stockpiles

Track where materials are stored, what is assigned to each job, and what remains available.

  • Yard and stockpile locations
  • Barrier and material counts
  • Job allocations
  • Remaining inventory

Daily Operations

Follow the work from planning to completion.

The system is designed to preserve the connection between the original job, the dispatched resources, the field activity, and the final results.

1

Create the Job

Record the contractor, project, location, dates, work requirements, and expected quantities.

2

Build the Dispatch

Assign crews, drivers, trucks, trailers, forklifts, materials, and supporting equipment.

3

Complete the Field Work

Track actual start and finish times, labor, quantities, equipment use, and operational notes.

4

Review the Results

Update job progress, material totals, equipment status, maintenance needs, and final task summaries.

Operational Visibility

Know what is assigned, where it is going, and what happened.

Dispatch and Jobs

Coordinate the Day

Dispatch should show more than a name and vehicle. It should establish the complete operational plan for the assignment.

  • Select the foreman and crew
  • Assign drivers to vehicles
  • Attach trailers and equipment
  • Select material and stockpile sources
  • Prevent duplicate equipment assignments
  • Review upcoming daily and weekly work
Field Reporting

Record What Actually Happened

Task summaries preserve the field results and help managers understand job progress, production, labor, and equipment use.

  • Actual start and finish time
  • Work quantities completed
  • Barrier and material counts
  • Employee hours and crew size
  • Equipment and operator information
  • Notes, issues, and follow-up needs

Asset and Material Control

Keep equipment ready and materials accountable.

Operational planning depends on knowing which resources are available, assigned, under repair, or located at another yard or jobsite.

READY

Fleet Readiness

See which trucks, trailers, forklifts, and machines are available, dispatched, assigned, out of service, or waiting for repair.

HIST

Maintenance History

Connect inspections, write-ups, repairs, recurring services, mileage, and maintenance decisions to the correct asset.

STOCK

Material Accountability

Subtract materials when dispatched, track quantities assigned to jobs, and update remaining stock after field work is completed.

Prototype Foundation

Built from lessons learned through FleetTrack Pro.

FleetTrack Pro is a Java desktop prototype used to explore the operational relationships between companies, employees, vehicles, equipment, jobs, maintenance, dispatch, and materials.

The prototype provides a working environment for testing workflows before the strongest ideas are refined into a focused commercial construction operations product.
