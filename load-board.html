Product Overview

Better freight decisions require better information.

Ghostline Load Board is being designed to present the operational details behind a load instead of reducing every opportunity to a pickup, destination, and rate.

VIEW

Clearer Freight

Present equipment requirements, appointment times, stops, commodity details, distance, and handling expectations in one organized view.

MATCH

Practical Matching

Help carriers find loads that match their equipment, location, operating area, available hours, and scheduling needs.

COST

Operating Context

Compare the offered rate against estimated fuel, mileage, time, tolls, repositioning, and other operating considerations.

Freight Marketplace

Built around the people who move freight.

The platform is intended to support the people posting freight, evaluating opportunities, planning equipment, and coordinating transportation.

  • Owner-operators
  • Small motor carriers
  • Company dispatchers
  • Fleet managers
  • Freight brokers
  • Shippers
  • Logistics coordinators
  • Transportation planners

Core Marketplace Features

Find, compare, and evaluate freight in one place.

The product is organized around the information required to determine whether a load fits the carrier, equipment, schedule, and operation.

SEARCH

Load Search

Search available freight using practical transportation filters.

  • Origin and destination
  • Pickup and delivery dates
  • Equipment type
  • Distance and radius
POST

Freight Posting

Give brokers and shippers a structured way to publish complete load information.

  • Stops and appointments
  • Commodity and weight
  • Equipment requirements
  • Special instructions
EQUIP

Equipment Matching

Reduce irrelevant results by matching freight to the carrier’s available equipment.

  • Dry van
  • Refrigerated trailer
  • Flatbed and specialty
  • Weight and dimension limits
LANE

Lane Comparisons

Compare opportunities by route, distance, offered rate, schedule, and destination market.

  • Rate per mile
  • Total trip distance
  • Pickup flexibility
  • Destination freight potential
TIME

Schedule Availability

Review whether the load fits the driver, equipment, appointment, and operating schedule.

  • Available pickup time
  • Delivery appointment
  • Estimated transit time
  • Equipment availability
PROFIT

Cost and Margin Estimate

Provide a planning estimate before the carrier commits to the load.

  • Fuel estimate
  • Deadhead mileage
  • Tolls and route expenses
  • Estimated remaining revenue

Marketplace Workflow

Move from available freight to an informed decision.

The workflow is designed to help users evaluate the full trip before accepting or assigning the load.

1

Search Available Freight

Enter the equipment, origin, destination, dates, and operating preferences.

2

Review the Requirements

Examine appointments, stops, commodity, weight, equipment, distance, and special instructions.

3

Compare Cost and Schedule

Estimate the operating cost, available time, deadhead distance, and destination value.

4

Contact or Assign

Move forward with the broker, shipper, carrier, or internal dispatcher using the complete load record.

Freight Intelligence

A posted rate does not tell the whole story.

A strong opportunity depends on the complete route, schedule, operating cost, destination, and equipment plan.

Lane Context

Understand the Complete Trip

The load should be evaluated as part of a larger transportation plan rather than as an isolated rate.

  • Distance to the pickup
  • Loaded mileage
  • Number of stops
  • Appointment restrictions
  • Route and toll considerations
  • Freight availability at the destination
Operating Estimate

Compare Revenue Against Expense

Cost estimates are planning tools intended to help carriers understand the factors affecting the trip.

  • Fuel consumption
  • Deadhead mileage
  • Driver and equipment time
  • Tolls and route costs
  • Maintenance allowance
  • Estimated remaining revenue

Marketplace Principles

Practical freight tools should reduce uncertainty.

Better structure and clearer expectations can help every participant make faster and more informed decisions.

INFO

Complete Details

Important requirements should be visible before a carrier spends time calling about the load.

FIT

Relevant Results

Search tools should prioritize opportunities that fit the user’s equipment, location, and operating plan.

PLAN

Decision Support

The platform should organize useful information while leaving the final transportation decision with the user.
