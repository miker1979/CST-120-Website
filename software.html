The project has been used to explore company data, employee management, driver assignments, vehicles, trailers, forklifts, equipment, jobs, task summaries, inspections, mechanical write-ups, maintenance, dispatch, and stockpile activity.

Development Principles

Practical software requires more than working code.

UX

Built for the User

Screens, navigation, forms, and workflows should reflect how employees perform their responsibilities.

DATA

Clear and Organized

Important information should be easy to locate, understand, update, verify, and review.

DEV

Practical Growth

Products can begin with essential functions and expand as genuine operational needs are identified and validated.

Product Development

From operational problem to commercial product.

Ghostline Software products are developed through a structured process that begins with the real workflow rather than a list of assumed features.

1

Understand the Operation

Identify the people, responsibilities, equipment, information, and decisions involved in the workflow.

2

Build the Core System

Develop the essential data models, screens, business logic, and operational functions.

3

Test the Workflow

Verify that the system supports real tasks and presents information clearly.

4

Refine and Expand

Improve the experience and add functions based on tested requirements rather than unnecessary complexity.
