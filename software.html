Built From Industry Experience

Software Designed Around Real Workflows

Ghostline Software is designed from direct experience in trucking, logistics, equipment management, field operations, and dispatch. The goal is to create tools that support workers instead of forcing workers to adapt to disconnected or overly complicated systems.

Operational Visibility

Give managers, dispatchers, drivers, and field teams access to clear and current information about jobs, equipment, personnel, and availability.

Practical Workflows

Design each system around the way work is actually performed, including dispatch decisions, equipment assignments, inspections, maintenance, and daily reporting.

Cleaner Information

Replace scattered notes, disconnected spreadsheets, and repeated data entry with organized records and consistent operational information.

FleetTrack Pro is a desktop fleet-management system designed around real logistics and transportation workflows.

The application supports company information, equipment records, drivers, jobs, dispatch activity, stockpile management, inspections, mechanical write-ups, and operational reporting.

The project continues to grow as new modules are tested and connected to the larger Ghostline software ecosystem.

Development Principles

Ghostline Software is being built around a few practical principles.

Easy to Understand

Screens and workflows should be clear enough for users to understand without unnecessary complexity or excessive training.

Designed for the Field

Software should reflect the realities of trucking, dispatch, field operations, changing schedules, and equipment movement.

Built to Grow

Each product is designed so additional modules, reports, integrations, and operational features can be added over time.

Building Better Operational Tools

Ghostline Software is in active development and will continue to evolve as projects move from prototypes into complete operational products.
