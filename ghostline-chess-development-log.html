Development Timeline

Documented milestones from the project’s first playable build.

July 2026

First Playable Windows Prototype

Ghostline Chess began as a focused C# Windows Forms project with a traditional board and a clean local two-player game loop. This version established the board model, turns, piece selection, movement validation, captures, and basic endgame detection.

Working Foundation

  • Complete 8 × 8 board
  • Local two-player turns
  • Legal movement and blocked paths
  • Captures and board reset
  • Check, checkmate, and stalemate

What It Revealed

The prototype proved the core idea was playable. It also showed that the game needed a stronger visual identity, clearer game-state feedback, move history, and more complete handling of special rules.

The original Ghostline Chess v1.0.0 presentation and first playable board.

August 2, 2026

Gothic Board and Complete Rules Milestone

The project was rebuilt around Haunted Echoes Studios’ visual identity. Traditional pieces became the Hallowed Saints and Damned Souls, while the interface gained the Ghostline Tome, Graveyard, custom artwork, themed messages, and clearer move and check indicators.

Engine Completion

  • Castling and castling-safety validation
  • En passant and all promotion choices
  • FEN import, export, validation, and counters
  • Insufficient material and stalemate
  • Threefold repetition and fifty-move rule

Presentation Upgrade

  • Custom transparent court sprites
  • Algebraic notation in the Tome
  • Captured pieces in the Graveyard
  • Runic legal-move markers
  • Crimson warning around a checked king

The current gothic interface after the complete rules-engine testing pass.

August 4, 2026

Layered Audio and Piece-Specific Feedback

The v7 development build established a working audio architecture for the board. Background atmosphere now continues while independent move, capture, environmental, and game-state sounds play.

Audio Architecture

  • Software mixer for simultaneous audio layers
  • Balanced 5:50 looping gothic background suite
  • Four randomized creaks with no immediate repeat
  • Deferred device startup to prevent launch freezes

Board Feedback

  • 24 routed move and capture cues
  • Six piece types across both factions
  • Bright ceremonial identity for the Hallowed Saints
  • Dark creature-based identity for the Damned Souls

The system is stable and playable; individual cues remain subject to track-by-track review.

Next Milestone

Curate the final sound set and strengthen visual feedback.

Planned work includes track-by-track cue review, spectral capture fades, improved runic indicators, release packaging, and continued separation of the chess engine from the interface.
