August 10, 2026 · Haunted Echoes Studios / Arizona Paranormal Project

Jerome ending architecture, resolver QA, and deeper historical spine

Jerome received its largest narrative-systems pass to date. The work focused on making the campaign endings deterministic enough to program while keeping the public-facing mystery deliberately incomplete.

Ending and cinematic architecture

  • Finalized independent outcome tracks for the entity, Elias, the eight miners, each investigator, and the town instead of relying on one blended ending score.
  • Completed a detailed final-campaign cinematic bible describing faction states, survivor outcomes, player deaths, chamber conditions, and town aftermath across the full ending ladder.
  • Locked the terminal town-collapse safeguards, including the absolute total-loss override, without exposing those exact ending conditions on the public game page.

Resolver and QA systems

  • Built a master ending resolver and QA matrix covering chamber integrity, sacred readiness, exact miner ledgers, Elias gates, player gates, town gates, covenant safeguards, and regression scenarios.
  • Separated campaign performance from narrative understanding so collecting large amounts of evidence cannot automatically unlock an ending the investigators do not actually understand.
  • Added sequential Revelation Gates that require meaningful story discoveries and location milestones before the highest ritual routes become available.

Canon refinement

  • Clarified the relationship between the eight 1928 miners, Elias, and the nine-position talisman so the historical containment system and the modern ritual system no longer conflict.
  • Expanded the historical spine beneath the 1928 mystery into a much older pattern while preserving the miners, Elias, real Jerome locations, talisman, and previously locked ending systems.
  • Kept the deeper origin mythology inside the private Canon Bible rather than publishing the answers on the website.

Website presentation

  • Rewrote the Jerome project page as a spoiler-conscious public preview.
  • Removed direct explanations of the true ending, the center talisman position, and Elias’s final role.
  • Reframed the project around buried history, unreliable voices, real Jerome locations, adaptive investigation, and consequence-driven endings.

Next: consolidate the revised historical spine into the next authoritative Canon Bible revision, continue resolver QA, and resume the Jerome field-reference/environment pass.
