August 2, 2026 · Haunted Echoes Studios

Studio Identity and Intro Experience Updated

The Haunted Echoes Studios artwork was updated with glowing eyes and a lantern. The website intro now defaults to sound on, provides a sound-off control, and preserves a click-to-play fallback for browser autoplay restrictions.

What improved

  • Refined the studio artwork with glowing eyes and a hand-held lantern.
  • Made the cinematic introduction request sound on by default.
  • Changed the control to clearly offer “Turn Sound Off.”
  • Kept a click-to-play fallback for browsers that block audible autoplay.

August 2, 2026 · Haunted Echoes Studios

Ghostline Chess Rules Engine and Gothic Board Published

The Hallowed Saints and Damned Souls interface, custom pieces, Ghostline Tome, Graveyard, FEN tools, and complete rules-engine testing were published to GitHub and documented on the company website.

What improved

  • Replaced the standard prototype presentation with the Hallowed Saints and Damned Souls visual system.
  • Added the Ghostline Tome, captured-piece Graveyard, FEN tools, and clearer game-state messaging.
  • Validated castling, en passant, promotion, checkmate, stalemate, repetition, insufficient material, and the fifty-move rule.
  • Added a crimson warning treatment for a king in check.
