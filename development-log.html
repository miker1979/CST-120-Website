August 15, 2026 · Haunted Echoes Studios / Ghostline Chess

Difficulty selection and visual-readability refresh completed

Ghostline Chess received a focused usability and presentation update designed to make legal moves easier to read and the computer opponent easier to approach without changing the complete chess rules.

  • Added Easy, Normal, and Hard AI difficulty choices.
  • Replaced small legal-move markers with full-square destination highlighting and a distinct capture treatment.
  • Added Oxblood Chapel, Crypt, and Moonlit Graveyard board themes.
  • Reorganized the interface so the board and status remain on the left while the Tome, Graveyard, FEN, AI, theme, and audio controls occupy a compact right-side panel.
  • Completed final selector, status-text, spacing, and audio-control polish after testing the updated layout.
  • Published the latest interface screenshot and feature summary to the Ghostline Chess website.

Result: the development build compiles cleanly and the refreshed visual system is complete.
